Selank Available for Research Use Only

(300 – 900 mcg daily, internasal spray, cycled)

Selank is a synthetic tuftsin-derived heptapeptide with the amino-acid sequence Thr-Lys-Pro-Arg-Pro-Gly-Pro. It was developed in Russia for intranasal administration as an anxiolytic and nootropic peptide. It is used clinically in Russia by intranasal administration to reduce anxiety and produce mild antiasthenic and nootropic effects. Benefits for stress resilience, attention, and working memory are likely.

💉💉Safety of short courses is likely. Long-term safety is plausible.

⭐⭐ Effective for anxiety. Likely effective for cognitive support and stress resilience.

Short-term safety is likely based on the favorable tolerability reported in small controlled human trials. Long-term safety is plausible based on Selank’s peptide biology, short-term clinical tolerability, and supportive animal evidence.

Dosing Patterns

Intranasal Selank produces acute central effects via direct transport to the central nervous system intranasal application. Community reports commonly describe subjective effects within 15–60 minutes.

Selank dosing is goal-dependent. Common dosing frequencies and cycle lengths are practice-derived combining Russian clinical practice and speculative community practice.

  • Situational stress: 150–300 mcg per administration, one to three times daily, for a total of 150–900 mcg daily.
  • Daily anxiolysis: 150–300 mcg per administration, twice daily, for a total of 300–600 mcg daily.
  • Cognitive support: 150–450 mcg per administration, once or twice daily, for a total of 150–900 mcg daily.

Safety signals exist from a 2015 clinical study where Selank was administered intranasally at 2.7 mg daily for 14 days, with some participants continuing through day 21.

Benefits

  • Anxiety reduction: Selank reduces anxiety without clinically significant sedation.
  • Improved stress resilience: Selank likely reduces behavioral and cognitive disruption during stress.
  • Cognitive support: Selank likely supports attention, learning, and memory, particularly under stress.
  • Neurotrophic signaling: Selank likely increases hippocampal BDNF expression. Resulting support for synaptic plasticity is plausible.
  • Immune modulation: Selank modulates cytokine and immune signaling.
  • Antiviral activity: Selank likely has antiviral activity against influenza viruses.
  • Enkephalin signaling: Selank plausibly prolongs endogenous enkephalin activity by inhibiting enkephalin-degrading enzymes.
  • Community reports: Improved calm, focus, and sleep are widely reported.

Potential Applications

  • Anxiety disorders: Selank reduces anxiety symptoms in generalized anxiety disorder and in studied phobic-anxiety and somatoform disorders.
  • Situational anxiety: Selank likely reduces acute situational anxiety.
  • Stress-related cognitive impairment: Selank likely supports attention, learning, and memory under stress.
  • Post-stroke recovery: Selank likely supports neurological recovery after stroke.

Cautions

  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding: Use is precautionary because safety during pregnancy and lactation is not established.
  • Unstable psychiatric conditions: Use caution in psychosis or bipolar mania because Selank’s central nervous system effects have not been adequately studied in these conditions.
  • CNS depressants and psychoactive polypharmacy: Interactions are poorly characterized, so concurrent use warrants caution.

Side Effects

  • Nasal irritation: Intranasal administration can cause temporary burning or irritation.
  • Sleep effects: Community reports describe sleep disturbance or vivid dreams, particularly with late dosing.
  • Activation: Community reports describe occasional temporary anxiety or activation.

Biological Mechanism

Selank is a tuftsin-derived heptapeptide with multimodal effects. After intranasal administration, direct transport to the central nervous system through olfactory or trigeminal pathways is plausible. Selank plausibly enhances GABAergic signaling through positive allosteric modulation and prolongs endogenous enkephalin activity by inhibiting enkephalin-degrading enzymes. It likely increases hippocampal BDNF expression and modulates monoamine metabolism. It also modulates cytokine signaling. Together, these effects plausibly support synaptic plasticity, neuroprotection, and regulation of anxiety and stress responses.

Stacking Selank and Semax

Combining Selank and Semax is widely reported in community practice. Because the peptides influence partly distinct but overlapping central nervous system pathways, complementary effects on anxiety, stress resilience, attention, and learning are plausible. Additive activation, sleep disturbance, or mood effects are also plausible. Specific combined doses, sequencing strategies, alternating-day schedules, and cycling patterns remain speculative.
